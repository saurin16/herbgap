    03 / METHODS & SOURCES

    How the atlas works

    Recording activity

    Count distinct dates with other vascular plant reports in each cell. Use CC0 iNaturalist observations via GBIF with reported coordinate uncertainty of at most 1 km.

    Survey shortlist

    Cells need at least three background report days and zero target reports. HerbGap combines nearby target records within 30 km with local recording activity.

    Source records

    GBIF / iNaturalist dataset · Natural Earth · EMA monographs for yarrow and ribwort plantain.
